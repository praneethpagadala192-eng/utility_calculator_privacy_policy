Privacy Policy

Utility Calculator

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  •  Package ID: com.aistudio.calculator.vqwxyz

Privacy at a Glance Utility Calculator works completely offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or advertising identifiers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Utility Calculator ("the App") handles your information. Your privacy is important to us, and the App is designed from the ground up to operate locally without requiring accounts, sign-ins, or cloud uploads.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store on external servers, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or precise/approximate location data
  • Usage analytics, behavioral tracking, or crash telemetry sent to third parties
  • Photos, contacts, files, or microphone/camera recordings

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide convenience across calculator tools (including General, Scientific, Currency, Unit, Discount, Loan, VAT, Date, Fuel, GPA, Health, Hexadecimal, Savings, and Tip calculators), the App saves your Calculation History (calculator type, formula/inputs, result, and timestamp) strictly in a private local database on your device.

This data never leaves your device unless you have Android's system-level device backup enabled in your Android settings, in which case Google's standard Android Auto Backup service may back up app data according to your device preferences.

4. Permissions & Offline Functionality

Utility Calculator requests no sensitive device permissions and performs all math and unit/currency calculations locally on your device.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The App does not include third-party advertising networks, marketing SDKs, or behavioral tracking libraries. We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

6. Your Data Controls & Deletion

You have full control over all locally stored calculation history at any time:

  • Open the History dialog from the top bar to delete individual calculation entries or tap Clear History to erase all saved records immediately.
  • You may also clear the App's storage in your Android device settings or uninstall the App to permanently remove all local data.

7. Children's Privacy

Utility Calculator is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, children's privacy is protected by design.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and inside the App's Privacy Policy viewer.
